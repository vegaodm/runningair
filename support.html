Frequently Asked Questions

How is my data stored?
Your running data is stored locally on your device using a secure SQLite database. We prioritize your privacy; your personal running history remains on your phone unless you explicitly export it.
Can I export my runs to other apps?
Yes! You can export your activities as GPX files through the app, making it easy to share your routes or import them into other fitness platforms.
Why does my GPS accuracy vary?
GPS accuracy can be affected by tall buildings, heavy tree cover, or weather conditions. Our app uses advanced filtering algorithms to provide the most accurate path possible.
I lost my data after an update. What should I do?
We design our updates to be seamless. However, if you encounter any issues during a version transition, please contact us immediately. We always recommend keeping regular backups via the export feature.
